# More Than Gold

A responsive, static EDUH4058 field-trip reflection. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with `python3 -m http.server 8000` and visit http://localhost:8000. No build or package installation is needed. Fonts load from Google Fonts, with local fallbacks.

## Photographs

Three original user-supplied photographs are included in `assets/`: the completed challenge scorecard, participants wearing marathon hats, and the personal encounter with Holly described in the brief. They were copied unchanged from screenshots supplied in the workspace. The solo athlete portraits are not used as substitutes for personal experience.

## Editorial basis

First-person prose paraphrases the user's supplied reflection; it does not invent speaker quotations. The participation spectrum expresses connections, not equivalent effort or a hierarchy of worth. Official sources linked in the site were checked on 7 September 2026 for the festival programme, marathon date and both Olympic histories. Personal attendance and activities are based on the user's account.

The first-person wording is ready for the user's editorial review. No deployment has been performed.
